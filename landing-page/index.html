Designed for Sensory Comfort

Engineered to eliminate cognitive fatigue, visual hyper-reactivity, and salience hijacking.

🛡️

Silent Element Collapse

Instantly hides target clickbait video cards and feed posts without visual flashes, flickering, or jarring layout jumps.

🛑

Sensory Shock Guard

Disables thumbnail hover autoplay and unexpected motion on YouTube, preventing sudden visual startle responses.

🔤

Calm Titles (De-Clickbait)

Automatically converts ALL CAPS titles into peaceful sentence case, stripping repetitive screaming punctuation and emojis.

🎨

Muted Visuals Mode

Applies a soothing 45% desaturation to thumbnails until hovered, transforming chaotic feeds into a calm, focused library.

💬

Dysregulation Shield

Collapses comment sections by default, shielding you from toxic flame wars and Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD).

⏳

Gentle Time Anchor

Subtle, non-intrusive breathing cues help combat ADHD time blindness without triggering anxiety or demand avoidance.
